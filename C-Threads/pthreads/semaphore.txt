    Sync primitivies (also mutex, cond var, monitors & barriers)

    unsigned int with wait(),post() ATOMIC operations
    wait() decrement semaphore if > 0 else wait
    post() increment semaphore

    semaphore don't take ownership like mutex so in semaphore, 
    different processes/threads can wait and post to a semaphore,
    in mutex the one who locks is the one whoe releses (ownership)
